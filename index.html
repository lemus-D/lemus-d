About

UNC Chapel Hill

I'm a computer science student at UNC Chapel Hill. Most of what I build sits where software meets the physical world — robots, cameras, and simulations of things that move.

I like problems where you can check whether the answer is right: does the detector find the person, does the arm reach the pose, does the orbit close. That's pulled me toward a lot of computer vision and a lot of numerical integration.

Outside of that I make things — augmented-reality demos for other people's research posters, bots that play games badly, and the occasional simulation I can't stop tuning.
