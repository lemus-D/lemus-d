About

UNC Chapel Hill

I'm a computer science student at UNC Chapel Hill. I split the year between Chapel Hill and New York.

I'm a maker first. If I can build the thing instead of reading about it, I will, which is how most of the work on this page started. On campus I write the code for one of the robotics clubs' robots.

Most of what I build is software that has to survive contact with the physical world: robots, cameras, and physics simulation. In practice that means a lot of computer vision and a lot of numerical methods.

I prefer problems with a checkable answer. The detector either finds the person or it doesn't. The orbit either closes or it doesn't. The simulation at the top of this page returns to its starting position within 0.007 units after a full period, and getting it there was most of the work.
