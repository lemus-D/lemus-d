Three Body Problem

Simulation Running above

A gravitational simulator with a position-Verlet integrator written from scratch. Seven presets, four of which are exact periodic solutions that return to their starting positions after one full period. The version at the top of this page is the same physics, ported to run in your browser.

  • C++
  • raylib
  • Numerical methods

About

UNC Chapel Hill · Class of 2028

I'm a junior at UNC Chapel Hill studying computer science, and I'll probably pair it with engineering, physics, or a second major in math. I haven't settled on which, mostly because I'd rather keep learning in several directions at once — I think of that as a permanent condition rather than a phase. I'm first generation; my parents came from El Salvador and Guatemala, I grew up in New York, and I'm at Carolina on the Gates Scholarship and the Robert B. Williamson Scholarship. These days I work as a computer vision and AI engineer at Blue Sky Robotics, I help my dad run his business and keep automating the parts of it that shouldn't need a person, and I still go back to my old high school to help their robotics team through build season, mostly on the code. Away from a screen I train jiu-jitsu, lift, cook, and walk more than is strictly necessary. I'm an optimist about where all of this is heading, which is most of the reason I build things in the first place.
